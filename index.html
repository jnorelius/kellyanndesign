About

A designer returning to the work that started it all.

Kelly Norelius studied Interior Design at CSUN and began her career at Kelly Wearstler, then with a handful of small boutique studios. Those years shaped a lasting point of view: spaces should feel composed, personal, and truly livable — never staged for someone else’s life.

After stepping away from that world for more than a decade, she is building Kelly Ann Design again — this time from Orange County, alongside her work in residential real estate. The portfolio from those earlier years is gone; the eye, the training, and the craftsmanship are not.

Past celebrity and private-client work remains under NDA. What we share here is the practice as it stands now: thoughtful, grounded, and ready for the next chapter.

Approach

Design that supports daily life — not just the photos.

Kelly’s work favors quiet materials, clear proportions, and rooms that hold up to real mornings, evenings, and everything in between.

See the home, then shape it

Because she also works in real estate, Kelly reads a property for both potential and feeling — what it can become, and how it wants to live.

Calm over spectacle

The goal is composure: soft light, honest materials, and layouts that reduce friction instead of adding drama.

Coastal, not themed

Southern California informs the palette and pace — airy, grounded, and specific to how people actually live along the coast.
